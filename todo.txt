 
=== Pro Feature ===

General/DefaultGeneral.js
Tag: Custom HTML wrapper tags (e.g., h1-h6).
Effect: Slider transition animations.
Left/Right Inner Gap: Spacing inside the slider items.
Slide Direction:(Horizontal and vertical) Control the movement path.
Arrow Style: Custom arrow designs.
Slide On MouseWheel: Scroll to change slides.
Arrow Follow Mouse: Interactive cursor-following arrows.
Slide on Mouse Drag: Drag-to-slide interaction.
Lazy Load Enable: Defer offscreen image loading.
Move From Edge: Spacing from the edge.


📂 General/VideoGeneral.js

Reset On End: Restart video upon completion.
Auto Hide Control: Hide controls during inactivity.
Mute: Video controls.
PIP: Picture-in-Picture mode.
Airplay / Download / Settings / Fullscreen: Extended media tools.

📂 General/GridGeneral.js
Position: Alignment of the pagination button.

📂 General/Post-query.js
Include Post/Product: Manually select specific items to show.
Exclude Post/Product: Manually select items to hide.
Exclude Current Post/Product: Prevent the current post from appearing in the feed.

📂 General/Carousel/Controls.js
Mouse Wheel: Scroll to move the carousel.
Grab Cursor: Enable dragging and show the grab icon.
 
📂 General/ThumbnailsGeneral.js
Direction: Layout flow of thumbnails.
Show Arrow/Navigation: Toggle arrows specifically for the thumbnails.

📂 Style/DefaultStyle.js
 

Margin & Padding: Box model spacing controls. 
Typography: Advanced font and text styles.
Colors & Hover Colors: Standard and interactive color states. 
Border & Radius: Box outlines and rounded corners.  
Animation (Delay / Duration): Entrance animations and timing. 


📂 Style/ThumbnailsStyle.js

Position: Placement of the thumbnails relative to the main slider.
Width & Height: Thumbnail dimensions.
Overly Color: Tint/color overlay on thumbnails.
Border: Thumbnail outlines.


📂 Item.js 

Button label: Text for call-to-action buttons.
Button Url: Link destination.
Open in new tab: Toggle to open the link in a new browser tab.
